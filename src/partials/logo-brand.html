FAQs

Frequently asked questions

  • Why is a logo important for my Forex company?

    A logo is the first visual symbol of your brand — it builds trust with traders and partners, helps your company stand out among hundreds of brokers, and creates an instant association with reliability. It also serves as the foundation for your entire brand identity.

  • What is brand identity and how is it different from a logo?

    A logo is just one element of the bigger picture. Brand identity includes your color palette, typography, graphics, tone of voice, and ready-to-use templates. Together, these elements create a consistent impression of your business across all channels and markets.

  • How long does it take to create a logo and brand identity?

    Depending on the package, logo design takes about 3 to 7 business days — from the first concepts to the final files. A full brand book is offered as an additional service and usually requires 14 to 21 business days to complete.

  • What deliverables will I receive?

    You’ll receive a folder with files containing your unique logo in multiple formats (PNG, SVG, AI) for use on websites, social media, and print. We also provide a curated color palette and typography selection, along with a mini-guide that includes the exact color codes used in your logo — essential for printing and for building a full brand book later. In extended packages, you’ll also receive a comprehensive brand book with detailed guidelines on logo usage, colors, and typography.

  • What do I need to do to order a logo from you?

    It’s very simple — choose a package and get in touch with us. We’ll send you a short branding brief to fill out, which helps us understand your goals and preferences. Once it’s completed, you’ll immediately have a clear view of the next steps and how the process will move forward.

  • What logo design packages do you offer?

    We provide three packages — Starter Logo, Professional Identity, and Premium Brand Identity. Each package offers a different scope of work and deliverables. You can explore the details of each package in the dedicated section of this page.

  • Do you provide copyright and usage rights?

    Yes, absolutely. Once the project is completed and approved, all copyright and full usage rights for the logo are transferred to you. If you’d like to have this confirmed in writing, we’re happy to sign any additional documents or agreements.

  • What does “revision round included” mean?

    A revision round is one cycle of changes that a client can request after receiving a draft design.

    You receive a draft (for example: a logo).

    You prepare a list of all the changes you’d like to make (update text, change colors, move elements, add or remove graphics). We implement all these changes and send you the updated version.

    Important: it’s not just one small tweak. One revision round can include multiple changes, as long as they are submitted together in a single list. If your package includes, for example, 1 or 2 revision rounds, they are free of charge. Any additional revision rounds are billed separately.

    If you need additional rounds beyond those included, we can provide them at an extra fee. The typical cost per extra round is $25–$100 (or more) depending on the complexity of the changes required.

  • Will my logo be unique and created from scratch?

    Yes. Every logo we design is created from scratch and tailored specifically to your business goals and market niche. We do not use templates — your logo will be 100% unique and exclusive to your brand.

  • Do you also create marketing materials or just the logo?

    Yes, in addition to logo design we also create a wide range of marketing and brand assets, including business cards, letterheads, folders, presentations, and social media designs. These can be part of extended packages or ordered as additional services.

  • Can you redesign or refresh my existing logo?

    Yes. We also provide logo redesign and refresh services. If you already have a logo but it feels outdated or doesn’t reflect your current business direction, our team can modernize it while keeping the essence of your brand. Simply contact our manager to discuss the details.

  • What makes your approach different from generic design agencies?

    Unlike generic design studios, we specialize in the financial and Forex industry. This means we understand the importance of trust, compliance, and global scalability in your brand image. Our focus is not only on creating a beautiful logo, but on building a cohesive, professional, and modern fintech aesthetic that helps you overcome market skepticism, gain credibility with traders, and scale internationally.
