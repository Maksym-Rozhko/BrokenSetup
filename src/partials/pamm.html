What is a PAMM Account

PAMM — Complex, Scalable, and Fully Automated

PAMM (Percent Allocation Management Module) is a sophisticated investment management model designed for large-scale operations

Investor funds are pooled in one master account managed by a trader. The system tracks trades and distributes results by each investor’s share.

Why PAMM Is More Advanced

By combining investor funds into a single master account, PAMM guarantees accurate distribution, clear reporting, and improved operational control for brokers.

Pooled Investment Structure

Full documentation preparation and licensing process management on your behalf.

Automated Profit Distribution

The system ensures accuracy and total transparency by automatically allocating profits and losses by share.

Enhanced Infrastructure

Designed for high-volume brokerage operations, includes risk controls, reporting and full investor oversight.

What is a MAM Account

MAM — Simple, Flexible,
Fast to Deploy

MAM (Multi-Account Manager) allows a trader to manage multiple client accounts simultaneously through a single trading platform. Instead of pooling funds, each investor keeps a separate account, while all trades from the master account are instantly copied to connected accounts.

Why MAM Is More Flexible

MAM lets brokers manage multiple accounts with full control —
fast, simple, and separate.

Independent Account Structure

Each investor keeps a separate account, allowing full transparency and individual risk control.

Instant Trade Replication

All trades executed on the master account are automatically copied to connected accounts in real time.

Fast Setup & Easy Management

MAM systems deploy quickly, set up easily, and need less infrastructure than PAMM.
