FAQs

Frequently asked questions

  • How long does the naming process take?

    Typically 5 to 10 business days, depending on project complexity, number of options, and language checks.

  • Where does the naming process start?

    It begins with a brand brief — analyzing your business, target audience, positioning, and communication style.

  • Should the company name match the domain?

    Ideally, yes. The name and domain should be aligned and reinforce each other. We help find the right balance between brand uniqueness and domain availability.

  • Can I develop names for several brands at once?

    Yes. We can create separate naming concepts for each business line or sub-brand within one project.

  • How is legal availability verified?

    Each name is checked for trademark availability within relevant jurisdictions and IP databases.

  • Do you check names across different languages?

    Yes. Every name undergoes linguistic and semantic screening to avoid negative meanings or pronunciation issues.

  • Does the name need to describe the company’s activity?

    Not necessarily. The goal is to ensure the name captures your brand’s character, values, and positioning, not just the industry.

  • Do you assist with trademark registration?

    Yes. We can provide legal support or consultation for trademark registration once a name is approved.

  • Who owns the rights to the name and domain?

    Full ownership is transferred to the client upon approval, including all credentials and domain rights.

  • Why choose BrokerSetup for naming?

    We specialize in brands within the financial and brokerage industries, where precision, trust, and compliance are essential.
